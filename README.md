# Restaurant-Data-Analysis
Data Analysis project on restaurant datasets including cuisine analysis, ratings analysis, price range distribution, geographic analysis, and restaurant chain analysis using Python and Google Colab.
# Restaurant Data Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes restaurant data to identify customer preferences, restaurant performance, cuisine popularity, pricing patterns, online delivery trends, and geographic distribution.

The project was completed as part of the Cognifyz Data Analysis Internship using Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, and Google Colab.

---

# Business Problem

Restaurant businesses need insights into customer preferences and market trends to improve their services and increase customer satisfaction.

This project answers questions such as:

- Which cuisines are the most popular?
- Which cities have the highest number of restaurants?
- How are restaurants distributed across different price ranges?
- How common is online delivery?
- How are restaurant ratings distributed?
- Which restaurant chains are the most popular?

# Key Insights

### 1. North Indian cuisine is the most popular cuisine.

North Indian cuisine appears in the highest number of restaurants followed by Chinese and Fast Food.

### 2. New Delhi has the highest number of restaurants.

New Delhi dominates the dataset with the largest restaurant concentration.

### 3. Most restaurants belong to Price Range 1.

Nearly half of all restaurants fall under the lowest price category.

### 4. Online delivery is available in fewer restaurants.

Most restaurants do not provide online delivery services.

### 5. Restaurant ratings are concentrated between 3.0 and 4.0.

The majority of restaurants receive average to good ratings.

### 6. Certain cuisine combinations receive higher ratings.

Some specialized cuisine combinations achieve excellent customer ratings.

### 7. Restaurant chains play an important role.

Cafe Coffee Day, Domino's Pizza, and Subway are among the largest chains in the dataset.

### 8. Geographic analysis helps identify restaurant clusters.

Restaurant locations reveal major food hubs and customer concentration areas.
